# Brag Plan: MetaMint (portfolio case study)

## What is this app?
MetaMint is a conversational AI money coach. The built layer is a working prototype of group expense tracking inside a WhatsApp-style chat.

## The angle
Same format as the Dolby and VeraAI videos. It opens on the Chicago Trip budget gone wrong, then asks the case study's question: "What if your money coach understood your financial life, not just your transactions?" Four cards of real phone footage answer it. The outro is the case study's own shift: "Not just split a bill."

## Hook (0-2.5s)
"Chicago Trip. My budget: $1,000." Then "Total: $1,286" and "oh no..." (the nine line items in the case study's budget hook add up to $1,286).

## Key moments
- The question (2.5-7s).
- Four cards of phone footage: Money Coach (Product Vision), Equal Expense Splitting, Itemized Expense Splitting, Personal Budget Tracking (all Working Prototype). The last one pays off the budget hook.
- Outro: "Not just split a bill." / "Everyday spending connected to the bigger financial picture".

## User flow worth showing
Post a receipt in the group chat -> MetaMint splits it (equal or itemized) -> see spent vs. budget in your own chat.

## Tone
- Preset: default (leaning polished); same direction as the other two videos.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f9f9f7 (paper) / dark for phone cards
- Accent: #1F6B4F (deep mint), #2E9E73 (bright)
- Text: #111111; display font Advercase; body system sans

## Share copy (draft)
Planned a Chicago trip with a $1,000 budget. Ended at $1,286. MetaMint is the money coach I designed for exactly that: it splits the bill right in the group chat and tracks your budget.

## Audio direction
Warm bed (vol-1) with sparse soft impacts on cards. Cards land on the beat grid at 7.02, 10.52, 14.02, 17.52; outro on the strong cue at 21.01. No audio-reactive.
