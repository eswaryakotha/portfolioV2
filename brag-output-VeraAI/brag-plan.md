# Brag Plan: VeraAI (portfolio case study)

## What is this app?
A case study of VeraAI, an AI-assisted design workflow for kitchen remodelers. It generates NKBA-compliant kitchen layouts, 2D/3D visuals, and a bill of materials and quotation.

## The angle
Same format as the Dolby Labs video. The case study opens on the problem: "Thirty days of decisions, and no deal." with the note "average time: 42 days". Then it asks the question the project answers: "How can we reduce a 30-day sales cycle to a single day?" The four JTBD cards answer it with real pilot footage.

## Hook (0-3s)
"Thirty days of decisions, and no deal." Then the correction "average time: 42 days".

## Key moments
- The question: "How can we reduce a 30-day sales cycle to a single day?"
- Four JTBD cards with real pilot footage: measurements to sketch, NKBA layout, 3D render, quotation.
- Outro: "1 visit to yes?" (from the hook diagram) and "10–15 NKBA‑compliant layouts generated".

## User flow worth showing
Enter measurements (rough sketch appears) -> NKBA layout -> 3D render -> bill of materials and quotation.

## Tone
- Preset: default (leaning polished)
- Creative direction: same as the Dolby video: quiet case-study film, one dry joke up front
- Interpretation: clean type on paper, dark footage cards, restrained SFX.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f9f9f7
- Accent: #081A4A (navy), #2F6BFF (bright, used for chips and glow)
- Text: #111111
- Display font: Advercase; body: system sans
- Strongest visual element: real pilot clips from the VeraAI website

## Share copy (draft)
Thirty days of decisions, and no deal. VeraAI generates NKBA-compliant kitchen layouts, 3D renders and a quotation. I built the frontend and the website.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM, CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende)
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Cards land on beat grid at 7.02, 10.52, 14.02, 17.52. The outro locks to the strong cue at 21.01.
- Audio-reactive: none (skipped, as in the Dolby video)
- SFX: sparse, soft impacts on cards, one click on the "42 days" note
- Restraint rule: nothing loud under text

## Storyboard
1. Hook — 0–3s — "Thirty days of decisions, and no deal." + "average time: 42 days".
2. Question — 3–7s — "How can we reduce a 30-day sales cycle to a single day?"
3. JTBD #1 — 7–10.5s — Capture kitchen measurements (clip 1)
4. JTBD #2 — 10.5–14s — Generate NKBA-compliant layouts (clip 2)
5. JTBD #3 — 14–17.5s — Produce 2D visuals using catalog items (clip 3, 3D render)
6. JTBD #4 — 17.5–21s — Generate a bill of materials and quotation (clip 4)
7. Outro — 21–25s — "1 visit to yes?", "10–15 NKBA‑compliant layouts generated", "VeraAI Technologies Inc. [2025 - 2026]"
Total 25s.
