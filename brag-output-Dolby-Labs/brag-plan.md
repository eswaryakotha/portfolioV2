# Brag Plan: Dolby Labs (portfolio case study)

## What is this app?
A case study of an AI-assisted audiobook creation tool ("Audiobook Studio") for independent UGC authors. Dolby Labs' brief was AI voice generation. The result was a tool that keeps the author's own voice.

## The angle
A plot twist. The brief said "AI narrates the book". The research said "Why is AI chasing after our artistic careers?" The video strikes out the brief, then shows the tool that shipped. It's the case study's own tagline, "(Spoiler: Not the way Dolby originally thought)".

## Hook (0-3.5s)
"Dolby Labs' brief:" then "AI voice generation tool". The phrase gets struck through on screen, as it is on the case study page.

## Key moments
- Quote: "Why is AI chasing after our artistic careers?"
- Four verbs land one by one: Annotate scripts / Guide narration / Fix recording mistakes / Enhance audio. Then "while preserving their creative control."
- Real product footage: AI Annotation Guide (JTBD #1) and the recording studio with enhancement toggle.

## Outro / punchline
"(Spoiler: Not the way Dolby originally thought)" with "Dolby Labs X Purdue [2023]".

## User flow worth showing
Highlight a phrase in the script -> AI recommendations (pronunciation, tone, narrator's guide) -> record and enhance audio (Original / Studio).

## Tone
- Preset: default (leaning polished)
- Creative direction: plot-twist case study, quiet confidence
- Interpretation: clean type on warm paper, a hard cut to dark product footage, one dry reveal at the end.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f9f9f7 (paper) / product footage dark
- Accent: #E07B39 (Dolby orange)
- Text: #111111
- Display font: Advercase (bundled in public/fonts)
- Body font: Geist -> falls back to system sans
- Strongest visual element: strikethrough "AI voice generation tool" + real Audiobook Studio screen recordings

## Share copy (draft)
Dolby Labs asked for AI that narrates audiobooks. Research said authors wanted to keep their own voice, so I built the tool around that.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM, CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, fades in 0.6s, fades out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Beat grid 3.02 + 0.5k. Strong cues 17.02, 18.52. Outro line locks to the 17.02 cue.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (extraction skipped; documented below)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Restraint rule: no loud hits under text; no repeated bright sounds
